Privacy Policy

NEXTMarketReaction Social Publisher · Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

NEXTMarketReaction Social Publisher (the "Tool") is internal software operated by the NEXTMarketReaction team (operator: NEXTMarketReaction, contact: nazarchychuk199908@gmail.com). It publishes NEXTMarketReaction's own original content to NEXTMarketReaction's own official accounts on TikTok, Instagram and X, and reads the performance metrics of that content.

The Tool is not offered to the public. Only the NEXTMarketReaction team connects its own accounts to it; no other person logs in to or uses the Tool. This policy covers the Tool only. The NEXTMarketReaction website has its own privacy notice.

2. Data the Tool accesses

When an authorized NEXTMarketReaction account is connected through the platform's official login (OAuth), the Tool accesses only:

PlatformDataPurpose
TikTokThe connected account's open ID and display name (user.info.basic); the IDs, publishing status and counts of views, likes, comments and shares of videos we published (video.list); access/refresh tokens Confirm we are connected to the correct account; upload our own finished videos to our TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload), which we then review, edit and post ourselves in the TikTok app; measure how our own videos perform
InstagramThe connected professional account's ID and username; IDs, links and insights (e.g. views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves) of media we published; access tokensSame purposes
XThe connected account's ID and username; IDs and metrics (e.g. impressions, likes, replies, reposts, link and profile clicks) of posts we published; access/refresh tokensSame purposes

The Tool does not collect data about other users: it does not read followers, messages, other people's profiles or content, or the identities of people who comment. Metrics are aggregate counts for our own posts.

3. How data is stored and protected

  • Access and refresh tokens and app credentials are stored only in the operating system's encrypted credential store (macOS Keychain) on the operator's computer. They are never written to source code, files, logs or reports.
  • Post IDs, links and metrics are stored in the Tool's local files on the same computer, for content analytics.
  • Media that a platform requires to download from a URL (Instagram) is uploaded temporarily to storage controlled by NEXTMarketReaction and deleted after publishing.
  • The sign-in redirect page on this website only forwards the one-time sign-in code to the Tool on the operator's computer. It stores nothing and contains no tracking. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's own privacy statement.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through the Tool. Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (for example, publishing a video to TikTok) and, for Instagram media, temporarily to our storage provider as described above. We do not use platform data for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than publishing and measuring our own content.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected or access is revoked; they are then deleted from the credential store.
  • Metrics of our own posts are kept while they are useful for our content analytics, and deleted on request.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the platform: TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), Instagram (Settings → Apps and websites), X (Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps).

6. Children

The Tool is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children's data.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this page if the Tool's data use changes, and change the "Last updated" date. Questions or deletion requests: nazarchychuk199908@gmail.com.
